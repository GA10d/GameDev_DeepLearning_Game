MNIST handwritten digit database
Authors: Yann LeCun, Corinna Cortes, Christopher J. C. Burges
Original: https://yann.lecun.org/exdb/mnist/index.html
Mirror and original-file MD5 checks: https://github.com/pytorch/vision/blob/main/torchvision/datasets/mnist.py
